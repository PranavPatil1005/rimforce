RAPID TELEMETRY FAQ
flight_takeoff

Where is my order shipped from?

All bespoke and forged monoblock rims are engineered and precision-packaged directly out of our primary automated Munich CNC and TUV inspection facility in Germany, with insured international air freight.

public

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We ship to over 85 countries with full customs handling, climate-regulated flight pallets, and guaranteed undamaged transit insurance via DHL Express Aviation and FedEx Aerospace logistics.

meeting_room

Can I visit a showroom in person?

Ateliers in Munich, Los Angeles, and Dubai accept private vehicle fittings by advance reservation. Contact our concierge desk or use the appointment form above to secure track or studio access.
