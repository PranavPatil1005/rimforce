## Brand & Style

This design system expresses ultra-performance luxury, precision CNC engineering, and unapologetic power. Tailored for bespoke aftermarket wheel connoisseurs, track drivers, and luxury hypercar owners, the interface balances raw mechanical intensity with refined editorial restraint.

The style unites **Tactile High-Contrast Minimalism** with **Subtle Cyber-Mechanical Accents**:
- Deep, light-absorbing obsidian voids anchor the experience.
- Laser-machined metallic lines, brushed titanium chamfers, and precise high-speed crimson telemetry flares provide visual tension.
- Product staging mimics high-end studio light boxes with rim-lighting reflections, specular micro-highlights, and architectural precision.
- Interactive states trigger sharp, decisive shifts reminiscent of paddle-shifters and track instruments—delivering immediate tactile confidence.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-spectrum contrast and luminous performance accents:

- **Deep Obsidian (`#0B0C10`)**: The primary foundation. Absorbs clutter and creates an infinite black backdrop that elevates forged metal finishes.
- **Carbon Canvas (`#12141A`) & Alloy Base (`#1F2833`)**: Tonal structural tiers for cards, panels, spec tables, and segmented viewports.
- **Precision Racing Crimson (`#E62020` / `#FF2A2A`)**: The primary interactive strike color. Reserved for high-value calls-to-action, fitment locks, active states, cart triggers, and tachometric alerts.
- **Brushed Titanium (`#C5C6C7`) & Billet Silver (`#EAECEE`)**: High-fidelity typographical hierarchy, subtle hairline borders, and polished surface accents.
- **Muted Graphites (`#454F59` / `#2B333E`)**: Structural dividers, disabled states, and inactive geometry.

Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) sparingly, strictly for primary hero titles and specular lighting effects.

## Typography

The typographic pairing enforces mechanical rigour through two complementary engines:

1. **Montserrat (Display & Section Headers)**: Dense, wide proportions with motorsport lineage. Used strictly in bold and extrabold weights for punchy headlines and tracked-out mechanical micro-labels. All small labels (`label-caps-*`) must render in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) to evoke chassis serial stampings.
2. **Inter (Interface & Data)**: Neutral, ultra-legible geometry for technical readouts, offset fitment tables, load ratings, and long-form editorial copy.

Keep body text locked to `#C5C6C7` or `#8E959E` on obsidian layers to reduce eye fatigue and maximize high-key product luminosity.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an uncompromising 8px mechanical grid:

- **Desktop (12-column layout)**: Max-width 1440px with `margin` at `3rem` and `gutter` at `1.5rem`. Technical parameter overlays, vehicle fitment pickers, and product imagery occupy calibrated split-pane modules (50/50 or 60/40 splits).
- **Tablet (8-column layout)**: Breakpoint at 1024px. Margins collapse to `2rem`, gutters maintain `1.25rem`. Complex wheel builder views reflow into stacked canvas viewports with sticky control docks.
- **Mobile (4-column layout)**: Breakpoint at 640px. Outer canvas margins contract to `margin-sm` (`1.25rem`) with `gutter-sm` (`1rem`). Technical specifications shift from inline comparative matrices into horizontal snap-scrolling cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a machined surface-stacking philosophy rather than soft, generic drop shadows:

- **Layer 0 (Core Void)**: Base screen background `#0B0C10`.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Chassis)**: Backplate color `#12141A` bounded by a 1px hairline border of `rgba(197, 198, 199, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Flyouts & Drawers)**: `#1F2833` with subtle drop shadow: `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` combined with a top-edge highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Performance Crimson Ambient Glow**: On high-priority cards, selected wheel finishes, and checkout triggers: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(230, 32, 32, 0.25)`.
- **Backdrop Frosting**: Sticky navigation overlays and quick-spec popovers utilize deep frosted glass: `rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, angular, and structural:

- Set to level `1` (Soft): Standard radii remain tight at `0.25rem` (4px), reflecting the sharp chamfers and CNC-milled edge tolerances of custom forged aluminum wheels.
- `rounded-lg` sits at `0.5rem` (8px) for master dialogue modules and cart trays.
- Round geometry (50% border radius) is strictly sequestered to wheel finish swatch selectors, lug-pattern indicator icons, and circular status markers.
- Pill buttons and soft bubble shapes are prohibited across all layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Track Red)**: `#E62020` solid background, text in white `label-caps-lg`, uppercase, font weight 700. Subtle chamfered corners (4px radius). Hover state shifts background to `#FF2A2A` with a radiant crimson glow (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 42, 42, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Billet Metal Outline)**: Transparent background, 1px solid `rgba(197, 198, 199, 0.35)`, text in `#EAECEE`. Hover scales border to solid titanium `#C5C6C7` with background fill `rgba(197, 198, 199, 0.06)`.
- **Ghost Action**: Monospaced red accent text with a 1px animated crimson underline expanding on hover.

### Product Presentation Cards
- **Construction**: Dark composite container (`#12141A`) outlined with 1px hairline titanium (`rgba(197, 198, 199, 0.12)`).
- **Visual Staging Area**: High-contrast matte black vignette staging wheel 3D renders or photography.
- **Spec Ribbon**: Displaying diameter, width, bolt pattern, and forge material with `label-caps-sm` styling on dark graphite pills (`#1F2833`).
- **Interactive State**: Elevates +2px on Y-axis; border transitions to crimson `rgba(230, 32, 32, 0.6)` with light perimeter glow.

### Chips & Finish Swatches
- **Finish Filters (Brushed Bronze, Matte Black, Gloss Red, Raw Titanium)**: Compact rectangular chips with 4px radius. Inactive: `#12141A` with `#8E959E` text. Active: Titanium border (`#C5C6C7`), `#FFFFFF` text, and a precision 2px red indicator strip underneath.
- **Physical Color Swatches**: 28px circular metal swatches encapsulated by a 1px border. Selected state adds a 2px outer crimson offset ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #0B0C10, 0 0 0 4px #E62020`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Vehicle Match & Custom Dimension Inputs**: Deep carbon backdrop (`#101217`), 1px structural border (`rgba(197, 198, 199, 0.2)`), inner horizontal padding `space-md`, height 48px. Text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Focus State**: Hairline border sharpens to `#E62020` with zero blur spread to maintain mechanical precision.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom angular 18px squares/diamonds. Checked fill is solid `#E62020` with a stark white icon.

### Bespoke E-Commerce Cart & Fitment Confirmation
- **Fitment Validator Badge**: Dynamic vehicle badge (e.g., "GUARANTEED TRACK FITMENT // 5x112 / ET35"). Dark emerald-crimson status indicators confirming offset compatibility.
- **Cart Slide-Out Chassis**: Obsidian glass (`rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.94)`) with an integrated CNC-milled weight calculator showing overall unsprung mass savings.
- **Order Call-To-Action**: Full-width high-vis crimson button paired with instant subtotal and forged build lead times.
